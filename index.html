Civic Technology

Software for Second Chances

Indiana's Second Chance Law (IC § 35‑38‑9) allows people to seal old, non‑violent records — unlocking employment, housing, and voting rights. But the process is a 3‑hour administrative bottleneck of statutory math, 92‑county docket searches, and 10 separate court filings. I'm building the tools to compress that to under 2 minutes.

⚖️

indiana-expunge-pro

Enterprise clinic workflow engine that batch‑processes multi‑client MyCase exports, evaluates 5‑tier IC § 35‑38‑9 eligibility in parallel, flags unpaid restitution traps, and generates 10 court‑ready Trial Rule 10 pleadings per client. Includes grant impact reporting for Legal Services Corporation and a one‑click ZIP archive of all packets.

IC § 35‑38‑9 Trial Rule 10 Batch PDF 100% Client‑Side
📊

reentry-dashboard

Zero‑knowledge, client‑side statutory deadline tracker and case vault. Auto‑computes Trial Rule 6(A) judicial day rolling, the 30‑day prosecutor response window, and 365‑day multi‑county filing clocks. Exports deadlines as RFC 5545 iCalendar files and includes a verified 92‑county clerk/prosecutor directory.

IndexedDB Vault Deadline Engine iCalendar Export
🔍

expunger

Chrome MV3 extension + standalone PWA that scrapes Indiana MyCase (Odyssey) court records directly in the browser, hooks into Knockout.js observables, deduplicates across dockets, runs the statutory rules engine, and compiles 10 court‑ready PDFs — all in‑memory, zero cloud.
